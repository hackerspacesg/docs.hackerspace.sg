## New business

### Proposal for a New 3D Printer (Snapmaker U1 3D printer)
#### Key Features of the Snapmaker U1:
- It is open source, making it easier to repair.
- It supports CMYK printing with four extruders, allowing for multi-color prints.
- It is less wasteful with filament compared to other printers when changing colors.
- It is more extendable and has a larger print space compared to the Bambu P1S.
- An optional top cover, which makes printing quieter and supports high-temperature materials, was discussed but was considered to be low priority as it was too expensive at an additional $150 USD.
 
#### Acquisition Options and Costs:
- A secondhand unit is available on Carousell for $800, but it might not be in perfect condition.
- Buying from the official Snapmaker website costs around $949 USD (approx. $1,200 SGD) including shipping.
- A local reseller on Shopee/Lazada lists it for $1,500 SGD.

#### Decision on Purchase:
- Crowdfunding Rewards: Ideas for rewards to incentivize contributors include offering print time (e.g., 10 hours), merchandise like t-shirts and stickers, and selling day passes. A previous successful campaign on "IndiGo" that offered similar rewards was referenced as a template (Projector).
- The team agreed to focus on crowdfunding before committing to ordering the printer, as the campaign may not reach the initial price target.
- The purchasing decision will wait until funds are raised, at which point discounts will be explored. The group may approach vendors for community discounts once a funding baseline is achieved.
#### Campaign Setup: The proposed plan is to start with a one-page public Google Doc describing the project, which will link to a Google Form for sign-ups. Open Collective was suggested for receiving donations.
- Advertisement copy will be drafted once the campaign setup is complete.
- Brian will create a more certain budget and send it to Valentine so one person can set up the fundraising.

#### Operations and Queue Management:
- The proposed model is to have users come to the space and operate the printer themselves after completing an onboarding session.
- An example operational flow was shared where users submit STL files and a Telegram bot tracks job status with photos. A person would monitor the printer and remove completed prints.
- It was noted that the printer may have an inbuilt camera for remote monitoring, but multi-color prints require manual filament changes, so full autonomy is not feasible.

#### Equipment and Logistics for 3D Printing
- Due to Singapore's high humidity, filament can spoil quickly. An airtight filament storage container and a specialized filament dryer box are required. Buying a new dryer box from Shopee for around $10-$12 is recommended.
- A discussion was held on what slicing software to use. The "Bamboo" 3D printer's software is proprietary and closed-source.
- A basic communal PC will be needed to control the 3D printer to standardize its use.

#### Proposed Pricing Structure:
- The discussion centered on setting a fair price, possibly requiring a day pass for access (Hardware day pass: $16, Normal day pass: $32).
- One proposal is to charge for machine time in addition to the day pass, while another suggests waiving machine time for day pass holders.
  - Members: Pay only for filament costs, not machine time. A suggested rate was around 4-10 cents per gram.
  - Non-members: Pay a machine time fee of $2 per hour, plus filament costs.
- It was noted that libraries offer free printing but have significant restrictions.
- The group agreed to finalize the specific numbers later but found the day pass model workable.
